Our Story

Built by people who've been on both sides of the journey

Neo Consult started with a simple observation: too many talented students were missing out on great opportunities abroad — not because they weren't good enough, but because the process was confusing, expensive to get wrong, and hard to navigate alone.

Today, our counsellors in Abuja and Leeds work as one team, supporting students from that very first conversation about "where should I study?" all the way through to settling into a new city. We keep our advice honest, our fees transparent, and our support available long after your offer letter arrives.

Our Mission

To make world-class education genuinely accessible — by giving every student clear, honest, personalised guidance from application to arrival.

Our Vision

A world where a student's postcode never limits their potential — where Abuja and Leeds feel like two neighbourhoods of the same community.

Our Values

What guides every recommendation we make

Honesty

We tell you when a course or country isn't the right fit — even when it's not what you hoped to hear.

Care

Every application belongs to a real person with real hopes — we treat it that way.

Clarity

No jargon, no hidden steps — just a clear plan you understand from day one.

Community

We connect students with each other and with alumni, so no one figures it out alone.
