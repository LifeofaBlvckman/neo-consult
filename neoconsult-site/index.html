Who we are

Supporting students into universities in the UK and beyond

Neo Consult is a study-abroad consultancy with offices in Abuja, Nigeria and Leeds, United Kingdom. We help students and families choose the right course, apply with confidence and settle in once they arrive.

It all starts with a free consultation. Your counsellor looks at your grades, budget and goals, then builds a shortlist of universities that genuinely fit — and the same team stays with you from that first conversation to your first week on campus.
