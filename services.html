What we do

Our solutions

Personalized Guidance

One-on-one support choosing your course, shaping your application and getting ready for life abroad.

Admission & Visa Counselling

End-to-end help with admissions and student visas — from documents to interview preparation.

Career Advice

Guidance that lines your course and country up with your strengths, interests and goals.

Accommodation Search

Help finding safe, affordable housing so you settle into your new city with ease.

University & College Admissions

We help you shortlist realistic, exciting options — then build an application that puts your strongest foot forward.

  • Course and university matching based on grades, budget and goals
  • Personal statement and reference letter guidance
  • UCAS and direct application support for UK institutions

Visa & Immigration Support

Visa refusals are usually about paperwork, not people. We make sure yours is right the first time.

  • Document checklists tailored to your destination country
  • Financial and sponsorship letter guidance
  • Mock visa interviews before the real thing

Test Preparation

Structured coaching for IELTS, TOEFL and SAT — built around your actual weak spots, not a generic syllabus.

  • Diagnostic assessment to identify your starting band score
  • Timed mock tests with detailed, one-on-one feedback
  • Flexible in-person (Abuja) or online sessions

Scholarship Guidance

Funding shouldn't be the reason a great student stays home. We help you find and win it.

  • Matching against university, government and private funding
  • Scholarship essay and interview coaching
  • Deadline tracking so nothing is missed

School Placement

For families exploring secondary and boarding school options, matched carefully to the student, not just the prospectus.

  • Shortlisting based on interests, pastoral care and budget
  • Entrance exam and interview preparation
  • Guardian arrangements for boarding students

Pre-Departure & Settlement

Getting the offer is only half the journey. We help with the practical side of actually moving.

  • Flight and accommodation guidance
  • Pre-departure orientation sessions
  • Check-ins from our Leeds team once you arrive in the UK
